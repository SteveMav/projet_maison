## Captured Decisions

- The tenant journey optimizes for fast triage before deep reading: location, price, bedrooms, freshness, and selective verification belong on listing cards.
- Verification remains narrow and reversible. It must never imply guaranteed availability, guaranteed accuracy, or a guaranteed offline transaction.
- WhatsApp is the external continuation boundary. Maison creates an attributable Lead before handoff and does not imply access to WhatsApp conversations.
- Public listing detail is full-screen on mobile and a right-side drawer on desktop.
- Warm-paper surfaces are the product canvas. Mint is reserved for trust-specific surfaces.
- Commissionnaires publish and maintain inventory from a phone. Moderators publish ordinarily, request corrections, reject or remove listings, and apply verification selectively.
