- MVP catalogue: clean listing cards, price, commune, bedroom count, real photos, and a detail page.
- MVP tenant flow: search and filter by commune, budget, and bedroom count; open a listing; contact a commissionnaire through a tracked WhatsApp handoff.
- MVP supply flow: a commissionnaire publishes a listing through a lightweight form with media.
- MVP trust baseline: selectively apply a verification badge and allow reporting of a listing or commissionnaire.
