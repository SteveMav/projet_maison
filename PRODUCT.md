# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Maison serves tenants in Kinshasa who need to compare credible rental options quickly on a mobile phone, then continue the conversation through WhatsApp without losing the listing context. It also serves commissionnaires who publish local rental inventory and need attributable, qualified leads rather than passive visibility.

The primary tenant task is fast triage: understand location, price, bedroom count, freshness, and trust signals without opening every listing. The primary commissionnaire task is to publish a useful listing from a phone, keep availability current, and see which listing generated each lead.

## Product Purpose

Maison is a rental-first marketplace for Kinshasa. It turns informal rental inventory into a structured search experience and turns tenant intent into traceable WhatsApp handoffs. It does not attempt to manage visits, negotiation, payment, or contracts in v1.

Success means tenants spend less time chasing stale options, commissionnaires see direct commercial value from maintaining quality listings, and trust signals remain narrow enough to be credible.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, reassuring, direct.

Maison should feel locally useful and quietly dependable. It should help people move quickly without creating urgency theater or overstating what the platform verifies.

## Anti-references

- Generic real-estate portals with dense walls of listings and weak hierarchy.
- Luxury-property aesthetics that feel detached from a practical rental search.
- Corporate fintech styling with navy, gold, and inflated trust language.
- Neon startup dashboards, decorative gradients, and excessive card stacks.
- Any interface that implies every listing is verified or guarantees an offline outcome.

## Design Principles

1. Make the first scan useful: location, price, bedrooms, freshness, and trust belong on the listing card.
2. Earn trust through specific signals: show selective verification, recent updates, reporting, and clear availability.
3. Keep the WhatsApp boundary honest: explain the handoff and preserve listing context without pretending to track the conversation.
4. Respect mobile attention: filters, forms, and commissionnaire actions must remain comfortable on a phone.
5. Use warmth with restraint: the interface should feel human and local while staying task-focused.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard usability for the responsive web surface.
- Keep tap targets at least 44 by 44 pixels.
- Never rely on color alone for verification, availability, errors, or selected filters.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and preserve task comprehension when movement is reduced.
- Use concise French interface copy and layouts that tolerate longer translated strings.
